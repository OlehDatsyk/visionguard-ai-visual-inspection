## Model

**YOLOX-Tiny** by Megvii: Apache-2.0, <https://github.com/Megvii-BaseDetection/YOLOX>. Weights are downloaded from the project's official release and are not in this repository. They were trained on COCO 2017. The COCO annotations are CC BY 4.0, and its images have varied Flickr licences. How that affects trained weights is unsettled, so review it for commercial use.

Ultralytics YOLO was deliberately not used, because of its AGPL-3.0 licence.
